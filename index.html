Our inquiry

Thought, identity, experience, and the self

Throughout Honors 241, Team Felix explores the relationship between thought, identity, experience, and the self. Through philosophical readings, written arguments, and collaborative discussion, we examine whether consciousness is something we possess, something we construct, or something that develops through our interactions with the world.

This portfolio documents our evolving understanding of these questions, preserving both our individual perspectives and the ideas we develop together.
